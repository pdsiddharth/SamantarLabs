05 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you asked.

Where does our data live?+

In-region by default, with single-tenant and on-premise options on the Enterprise plan. We sign a data processing agreement before any pilot begins.

Does this replace our records system?+

No. It sits alongside it and reads from it. Integrations to common hospital and ERP systems are part of the deployment.

How much training will our team need?+

Front-desk staff are usually working unassisted after a single session. The two-week parallel run gives everyone time to learn without pressure.

What does the AI actually do?+

It predicts wait and service times, flags queues about to overflow, and drafts routine messages your staff would otherwise type. Decisions stay with people.
